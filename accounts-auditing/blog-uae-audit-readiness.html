How to prepare your business for an audit

A practical checklist for getting your financial records, controls and supporting documents ready.

Audit preparation is easier when records and responsibilities are organized before fieldwork begins. A clear preparation process can reduce delays, make information easier to verify and help management address issues early.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm that the accounting records are complete and reconciled.
  • Organize invoices, contracts, bank records and supporting schedules.
  • Review unusual or high-value transactions and keep clear explanations.
  • Document key internal controls and who is responsible for them.
  • Prepare a list of outstanding questions or accounting matters for discussion.
